TILT10

Ten floors down. Twelve balls. Every ball you lose stays in the shaft.

  • You're looking down the shaft. Each floor is a board. Drop through its hole to dive to the next one.
  • The ball rolls toward your cursor. On a phone, hold your finger on the screen. The further away you point, the harder it rolls.
  • Dead balls stay where they stopped. They fill spike pits, hold plates down and add weight to glass.
  • Each ball has 60 seconds for the whole descent. When time runs out, its body stays where it is.
  • Want it to stop sooner? Click (or Space; two fingers on a phone) and it stops right there.

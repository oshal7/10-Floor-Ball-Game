Ten floors down. Twelve balls. Every one you lose stays behind.

  • Tilt your phone to roll the ball down the shaft.
  • Dead balls stay where they died. Their bodies fill pits, hold plates down and smash glass.
  • ⚓ Anchor gives up the current ball on purpose, and its body stays where it settles.

Desktop: arrow keys / WASD to tilt, Space to anchor. No sensor? Drag on the screen.
